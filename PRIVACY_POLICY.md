Sanatani Bhakti ("the app") is operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy
explains what data the app handles and why.

## Short version

The app has no accounts and no login. Your devotional activity — punya points,
streak, favourites, jaap count, chosen language — is stored **only on your own
device** and is never transmitted to us. We do not operate any server that
receives your data.

The one exception is advertising: the app shows ads from Google AdMob, and
Google receives device data in order to serve them.

## Information stored on your device only

The following is saved locally on your phone and never leaves it:

- Punya points, prayer streak, and total jaap count
- Favourite mantras
- Selected language and rashi
- Whether you have completed the first-run language selection
- Whether you have purchased Remove Ads

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. We have no copy and cannot restore it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may
collect and process:

- Your device's **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier)
- Device information such as model, operating system version, and language
- Approximate location derived from your IP address
- Interactions with ads shown in the app

We do not receive this data and cannot identify you from it. Google's use of it
is governed by Google's own privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

**Your controls:**

- **Reset or delete your advertising ID** in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland**, you are asked for consent before
  personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in the
  app via **Profile → Ad privacy settings**.
- **Remove ads entirely** by making the one-time Remove Ads purchase. After
  that, no ad is requested and no ad-related data is collected.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers one optional one-time purchase, **Remove Ads**. All devotional
content is free to everyone and is not affected by this purchase.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your
card details, billing address, or any payment information. The app only learns
whether the purchase is active, so it knows whether to hide ads.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not track your precise location.
- We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, other than the
  advertising data Google processes as described above.
- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is not
  directed at children.

## Device permissions

The app requests permissions only at the moment a feature needs them:

| Permission | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Photos / Media (write) | When you save a wallpaper | To write the image into your gallery |
| Audio (write) | When you save a ringtone | To write the audio file to your device |
| Advertising ID | Automatic | Used by Google AdMob to serve ads |

Declining any of these leaves every other part of the app fully usable.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for
us to export or delete on request. To remove everything the app has stored,
uninstall it, and reset your advertising ID in Android Settings.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may withdraw ad consent at any time via
**Profile → Ad privacy settings**.
